1. Recording rule

Each significant public Beta retains an identity independent of Grammar and IR version numbers. The B1, B2, B3… series identifies experimental realizations and does not replace normative Language versioning.

A Beta may be deployed without being integrated. Only an evidenced promotion can relate a specific Beta to a sovereign production commit.

4. Open proposals for a later Beta

The following proposals retain material future work identified during B1. Their registration prevents loss of that work, but does not constitute an implementation commitment or automatically expand scope.

  • LAB-P001 · Integrated development environment

    State: PROPOSAL

    Projects and multiple files, tabs, project tree, navigation across sources and symbols, execution, IR and trace inspection, local saving and reuse of the sovereign compiler API. The interface must not introduce an independent second parser.

  • LAB-P002 · Edit state and modified-file marker

    State: PROPOSAL

    Distinguish a loaded example from subsequently modified content, for example through file.svp * or an equivalent modified-state indicator, later integrated into the development-environment model.

  • LAB-P003 · Structured diagnostics and localizable presentation

    State: PROPOSAL

    Separate diagnostic code, position or span, structured arguments and presentation text. Diagnostic language must not alter the source profile or the identity of the error.

  • LAB-P004 · Persistent source-profile provenance

    State: PROPOSAL

    Retain, per unit when applicable, profile identity and version, catalogue and grammar without altering source_sha256. Compilation identity must distinguish source bytes from the linguistic dependencies that interpreted them.

  • LAB-P005 · Multi-source and module infrastructure

    State: PROPOSAL

    Study projects or modules composed of multiple source units and close profile/version compatibility before asserting composition. B1 does not promise ES/EN mixing across modules or an already-realized multi-source semantics.

  • LAB-P006 · Editing tools derived from the canonical catalogue

    State: PROPOSAL

    Syntax highlighting, completion and navigation must derive from the same canonical catalogue or generated artifacts, avoiding side vocabularies that may diverge from the compiler.

  • LAB-P007 · Versioned evolution toward additional linguistic profiles

    State: UNDER STUDY

    Preserve the possibility of studying new surfaces without retroactively reserving words that would break earlier programs. Portuguese, French, Italian and German were identified as engineering-viability candidates, not as constituted profiles or a committed implementation order.

  • LAB-P008 · Static-footprint strategy as profiles grow

    State: UNDER STUDY

    If table growth makes the WebAssembly footprint material, compare distribution or loading strategies without introducing remote translation, active databases or external services able to decide meaning.

  • LAB-P009 · Visual Unicode security

    State: PROPOSAL

    Explicitly govern invisible or directional Unicode controls and presentation risks in code, strings and comments before making visual-security guarantees for the ES/EN experience.

  • LAB-P010 · Specific trajectory/Trajectory parity review

    State: CHECK PENDING

    Before broadening equivalence claims between realizations, perform a specific reproducible check of the dual grammatical use of trajectory and Trajectory. This entry records a pending check; it does not by itself declare a current defect.

5. Proposal-continuity rule

A proposal that survives several Betas retains its identifier and state. The planned states are PROPOSAL, UNDER STUDY, READY FOR PROTOTYPE, IN BETA, DISCARDED and PROMOTED. A discarded proposal must retain its technical reason; a promoted proposal must link the integration evidence.
